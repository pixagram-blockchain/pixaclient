    Class NftAPI

    Native NFTs, read from the chain (database_api) and acted on with nft_operation.

    Every action is signed with the active key of account, its actor. The market summary of an NFT page, its history and the lists by creator, owner and price come from Pixamind (PixamindAPI.getNfts and the other …Nft… methods); this helper is what the chain holds at head block, which is what a purchase is checked against.

    find

    • find(ids: string[]): Promise<{ editions: ChainNftEdition[]; nfts: ChainNft[] }>

      Up to 100 NFTs or copies by id: @author/permlink/0 for an NFT (with its reserve), @author/permlink/N for the NFT with its copy N. Ids that name nothing are left out, so the call also tells which posts are minted. Each NFT and each copy is answered once, in the order first asked; a copy that was never offered nor moved is answered as its creator's.
